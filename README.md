# Mental Health AI Support & LLM Evaluation

An MSc Artificial Intelligence for Business project exploring AI-assisted mental health support, wellbeing resources, and the evaluation of large language model responses.

## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of my MSc Artificial Intelligence for Business programme at the National College of Ireland.

The project combines a mental health support web application with research into the use of large language models (LLMs) for mental-health-related conversational responses.

The research evaluates responses generated by ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude using NLP-based evaluation metrics including BLEU, METEOR, and ROUGE-L.

## Key Features

- Mental health support resources
- Breathing exercises
- Journaling functionality
- Positive affirmations
- Inspirational quotes
- AI chatbot research and evaluation
- Comparative evaluation of LLM-generated responses

## Research & Evaluation

Three large language models were evaluated using mental-health-related prompts:

- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- Claude

Generated responses were compared against reference responses using:

- BLEU
- METEOR
- ROUGE-L

The evaluation investigates the usefulness and limitations of textual similarity metrics when assessing open-ended emotional-support responses.

## Ethical Considerations

This project is an academic research and software project and is not intended to provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional mental-health advice.

AI-generated responses may contain errors or inappropriate information. Mental-health applications therefore require appropriate safety mechanisms, human oversight, and clear boundaries around the role of AI.

## Limitations

The evaluation metrics used in this project measure textual similarity and do not fully capture important characteristics of mental-health support, such as:

- Empathy
- Emotional appropriateness
- Contextual understanding
- Safety
- Factual correctness
- Quality of supportive communication

Therefore, quantitative metric results should be interpreted alongside qualitative analysis.
